# stroh_2019: grade C

Stroh M, Sagert J, Burke JM, Apgar JF, Lin L, Millard BL (printed "Miliard" in the supplement), Kavanaugh WM. Quantitative Systems Pharmacology Model of a Masked, Tumor-Activated Antibody. CPT Pharmacometrics Syst Pharmacol 2019;8(9):676-684. DOI 10.1002/psp4.12448, PMC6765697.

**Grade C, one line:** the model runs and was verified entry by entry against the supplement matrices, 7 of 10 monkey (Fig 3) lines pass, but only 14 of 35 targets pass as published (18 of 35 with the one readout fix, flagged circular). The human Fig 4 lines for the masked variants fail; Fig 4 used an unstated human kcleave (Table 2 gives only `< 3e-7 1/s`). The sensitivity scan shows about 1e-7 would match, but that value is not stated in the paper and choosing it by agreement would be tuning, so it is not used.

## Model

Six molecular forms per compartment (arm states c = mask closed, o = mask open ("breathing"), m = mask cleaved off; intact c_c, mono-cleaved c_m/o_m, dual-cleaved m_m = parental mAb), plus nine target-bound complexes, free target R, and terminal pools. Three compartments: plasma (central), periphery, tumour. States are concentrations in nM; rate constants stay in 1/s as printed and the ODE clock is days (factor 86400).

Per arm: `c -> o` at kopen, `o -> c` at kclose (Kmask = kclose/kopen), `c, o, oR -> m, m, mR` at kcleave (compartment specific); `o or m + R -> oR or mR` at kon1 (first arm) / kon2 (second arm); unbinding at koff1; complexes internalised at kendo into `tmdd_o`/`tmdd_m` pools; free R decays at kendo and is synthesised at ksynR/V; unbound forms eliminated at kel (plasma, periphery) and exchanged plasma-periphery (k12, k21) and plasma-tumour (k13, k31, amount based so that C_tumour/C_plasma -> p). Dose is an IV bolus into plasma (`pb_c_c` for Pb-Tx, `pb_m_m` for mAb0,0). Parameters: Table 1 (monkey), Table 2 (human), each commented in `model.jl`.

**Structural verification.** `supplement_check/check_matrices.jl` builds the first-order matrix A1 and second-order matrix A2 from the reaction list in `model.jl` with the supplement's own parameter list and compares with the transcribed supplement matrices: 234/234 A1 and 84/84 A2 entries identical. So the reaction network is the authors', including the quirk that the oR_m complex is internalised into the `tmdd_o` pool.

## Fixes

The as-published run and the fixed run both use fixes 1 and 2 (they are needed for the model to run at all, same values in both runs); only fix 3 changes the simulated curves.

1. `molecular_weight_150kDa` (`:missing`): doses in mg/kg are converted to nM with MW = 150 kDa. Evidence: the paper gives no molecular weight; 150 kDa is the standard IgG1 mass (the Pb-Tx prodomain makes the true mass somewhat higher). Consistency check only: 4.5 mg/kg x 70 kg / 150 kDa / 2.6 L = 808 nM, the ~800 nM of Fig 4a at t = 0.
2. `human_V2_2.6L` (`:missing`): human peripheral volume 2.6 L (not in Table 2). Evidence from the paper's own tables: Table 1 monkey receptor concentration 9.0e-5 nmol/s / (1e-4 1/s * 0.1 L) = 9 nM; the Results assume equal normal-tissue receptor concentration in human; Table 2 ksynR peripheral 2.3e-3 nmol/s / (1e-4 * 9 nM) = 2.56 L, i.e. V1.
3. `unbound_readout` (`:from_target`, circular): the paper does not define what "Total Drug" sums in periphery and tumour (Fig 4b, 4c); neither reading is the published one. The as-published run sums unbound + target-bound forms (same as for plasma); the fixed run sums the six unbound forms. The unbound reading was chosen because it matches Fig 4b,c for mAb0,0 and 10x (for example mAb at about 1e-2 nM in tumour, far below the 270 nM target level of Table 2), selected by comparison with the figure. With it the mAb and 10x curves in b and c go from rms 0.07-0.12 to 0.001-0.009 (pass). It does not change the grade (still C, 17 curves fail).

No model parameter was fitted or chosen by comparison.

## Issues found in the paper

- Table 2 gives kcleave only as `< 3e-7 1/s` ("assumed same as monkey"); the value used for Fig 4 is not stated, and it matters (table above). Table 1 and Results use 3e-7 as the value carried forward for Fig 3.
- Absolute breathing rate constants are not given (only Kmask = kclose/kopen); the split used (kopen = 1.16e-2 1/s, kclose = Kmask * kopen) comes from the supplement parameter list (Kmask 20 there).
- Molecular weight for mg/kg to nM is not stated (150 kDa used; reproduces the ~800 nM at t = 0 of Fig 4a).
- Human peripheral volume V2 is missing from Table 2 (2.6 L inferred from ksynR_periph 2.3e-3 nmol/s = 9 nM * 2.6 L * kendo, with the monkey receptor concentration "assumed equal").
- Table 1: koff1 = 2e-3 1/s, but the note says koff1 = kon1 * Kapp and Kapp = 1.0 nM, kon1 = 1e-3 gives 1e-3. (2e-3 is also what the supplement uses.)
- Table 2 notes "10-fold increased kcleave in the tumor" only in the Discussion, as the scenario of Fig 4d,e; whether the same fold applies to all of Fig 4 is not stated. The Discussion's "10-mg tumor" is presumably 10 g (V3 = 0.01 L).
- The paper does not define what "Total Drug" sums in periphery and tumour (the published readout is undefined; see Fixes) and the y-label of Fig 4d,e ("Total Drug (nmol)") is really cumulative internalised drug.
- Initial receptor level is not stated (steady state ksynR/(kendo V) assumed).
- Supplement s003: the printed `k` is described as zero-order rate constants but is a 32-entry parameter list; the real zero-order vector (receptor synthesis) is not printed; text says 52 states, the matrices have 55 (three receptor pools incl. an inert central one); the matrices are one parameter snapshot that differs from Tables 1-2 (Kmask 20, kcleave 1e-6 central/peripheral and 1e-5 tumour, k13 2.5e-9, k31 4.4e-6, ksynR tumour 2.8221e-5 vs 2.7e-4 in Table 2). Table 2's k13 = 1.9e-8 and k31 = 1.0e-5 are mutually consistent with Q = 1e-5, p = 0.5, V1/V3 and give C_tumour/C_plasma = p only in amount-based form, whereas the supplement coefficients (ratio 5.7e-4) do not; using them makes Fig 4c far worse (sensitivity).
- Author surname printed "Miliard" in the supplement and "Millard" in the article metadata.

## What would raise the grade

1. The systemic and tumour kcleave (and the tumour fold) actually used for Figure 4 (and 3), or the authors' KroneckerBio parameter file for the figures. With about 1e-7 Fig 4 would pass 24/25 (not credited).
2. A statement of what "Total Drug" sums in periphery and tumour (would make the readout documented rather than selected by comparison).
3. Molecular weight, breathing rates, V2 and the initial receptor level.
4. Higher resolution figures (the 709 px JPEG limits the digitisation to about 1 % of the axis).

## Review

Independent review, 2026-11-04. The review argued for grade D because the molecular weight (150 kDa) has no source in the paper. Final decision: C, under the documented gap-filling rule (a gap filled from a standard physical or physiological constant such as IgG about 150 kDa, or derived from the paper's own tables such as human V2 from equal receptor concentration, is a valid `:missing` fix with evidence; D only when an essential value has no such source). The review also found the attribution of the Fig 4 misfit to kcleave overstated and the Fig 3 coverage statement wrong.

Fixes made after review (one round):
1. MW 150 kDa and V2 2.6 L are explicit `:missing` Fix entries with evidence, applied in both runs (as published and with fixes), stated above.
2. The kcleave wording (top and Sensitivity) now says the value is unstated and that matching it would be tuning.
3. Fig 3 coverage corrected; contaminated early marker points removed by a documented `min_x` rule in `digitise.toml`; traces regenerated; no pass/fail change (14/35 and 18/35).
4. The "Total Drug" readout stays `:from_target`; the report no longer claims the published readout includes or excludes complexes (it is undefined).
